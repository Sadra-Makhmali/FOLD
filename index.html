How it plays

Fold. Match. Bloom.

Every puzzle is one sheet of paper and a handful of lights. There is no timer and nothing to lose.

  1. 1Fold

    Grab the sheet and drag it over itself. The crease follows your finger and settles onto the grid with a soft tap.

  2. 2Match

    When two lights of the same colour land on each other, they join. Line up several in a single fold for a chain.

  3. 3Bloom

    Connect every light and the sheet folds itself into an origami figure that flies off to join your world.

The game

Small folds, a whole world

FOLD is a puzzle game, a story and a place to rest. Here is what is inside.

The fold

A fold that feels like paper

FOLD is built around one gesture. Touch the sheet anywhere and drag: the crease forms halfway between where you grabbed and where your finger is, just as it does with real paper. The flap lifts and follows you, showing its back face, its shading and the shadow it casts.

  • Creases settle onto the grid with a gentle haptic tap.
  • Let go past halfway to fold, or earlier to change your mind.
  • A fold that cannot happen resists softly and shows you why.
  • Undo any fold, as many times as you like.

The world

Every solved sheet becomes origami

The sheet you just folded does not vanish. It gathers itself into a crane, a boat, a lantern or a fox, and travels to a floating island that has been waiting for it. Cranes circle overhead. Boats drift on the water. Bridges, shrines and trees rise from faint outlines into full colour as you play.

It is the same paper, from puzzle to world. What you fold is what you keep.

Beyond the story

Endless Weave and Daily Discovery

The Endless Weave is a journey with no last page: expeditions of five freshly generated puzzles, each expedition with its own artifact and a fragment of lore. Every puzzle is checked by the game's own solver before you see it, so there is always a way through.

Daily Discovery is one small puzzle a day, the same one for every player on that date. It is there if you want it, and it never nags.

  • Unlimited expeditions, five puzzles each.
  • Six flavours of puzzle, from torn paper to chains of light.
  • A new Daily Discovery every day, entirely optional.

The journey

Three realms, one quiet story

Lumi, a small spirit made of light, found you in the dark. Together you follow the old threads through three realms, 45 hand-tuned puzzles and 12 chapters, gathering memories for the Memory Atlas along the way.

  • The Forgotten Garden

    Where the first threads were tied

    An ancient bridge, a sakura tree and floating lanterns wake up as you learn to fold.

  • The Crystal Ocean

    A sea of glass between the fragments

    Glass boats, a tide lighthouse and a pearl whale, with sealed seams and creases that bend only one way.

  • The Sleeping Forest

    Roots that hold the world in place

    Evergreens, a fox shrine and the Heart Tree, where rooted paper and chains of light change how you fold.

Everything was quiet for a very long time. Then you arrived, and something small began to glow.

The promise

Free, and quiet about it

FOLD costs nothing and asks for nothing. It was made to be a calm place, so everything that usually interrupts a mobile game was left out.

  • No ads

    Not one. No banners, no videos, no sponsored anything.

  • No purchases

    Nothing to buy, unlock or subscribe to. The whole game is yours.

  • No timers

    No energy, no lives, no waiting. Play for a minute or an evening.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for. Open the game and fold.

  • No data collected

    No analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays with you. Read the privacy policy.

  • Works offline

    On a plane, in the mountains, anywhere. No connection needed.

For everyone

Made for every pair of hands

A calm game should be calm for everyone. FOLD includes options for the different ways people see, hear and touch, and each one is a single switch in Settings.

  • Shape symbols on lights. Every colour carries its own shape (drop, petal, star, leaf, moon), so colour is never the only clue.
  • Fold from a list. Choose any possible fold from a list instead of dragging. It is always available with VoiceOver.
  • High contrast. A clearer board with stronger definition.
  • Reduce motion. Quieter transitions throughout the game.
  • Sound, music and haptics. Each can be switched off on its own.
