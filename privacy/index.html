The short version

  • FOLD collects no personal data. None.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs in the game.
  • There is no account. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.
  • Game Center and the daily reminder are optional, stay off until you turn them on, and are handled by Apple on your device.
  • The game never needs an internet connection.

Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the iPhone game FOLD: Lumi's Paper World (“FOLD”, “the game”) and to this website, foldgame.app (“the website”).

FOLD is made and published by Sadra Makhmali, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to sadramakhmali.sm@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, share or sell any personal information through the game. FOLD contains:

  • no advertising of any kind;
  • no analytics and no usage tracking;
  • no third-party software development kits (SDKs);
  • no account, sign-up or login;
  • no in-app purchases;
  • no servers operated by us.

FOLD works fully offline. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, FOLD saves a small amount of information on your iPhone:

  • Your progress: the levels you have completed, the origami you have folded, the memories and achievements you have unlocked and the state of your world.
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, accessibility options and whether the daily reminder is on.
  • Simple play statistics: for example the number of folds you have made, hints used and time spent folding. These are used only inside the game, to show your journey and to gently adjust the difficulty of generated puzzles.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by choosing Reset all progress in the game's Settings, or by deleting the app.

iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for FOLD on your device, your saved progress may also be stored in your own private iCloud storage, so that it can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone. Like the data of most apps, it may also be included in backups of your device.

This storage is provided by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. You can turn iCloud off for FOLD, or delete this data, in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Game Center (optional)

FOLD can connect to Apple's Game Center so that your achievements appear in your Game Center profile. This is off until you choose Connect Game Center in the game's Settings.

If you connect, the game reports the achievements you earn to Game Center. Game Center is operated by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center profile, friends and activity are managed by Apple, not by us, and we do not receive or store your Game Center identity on any server. You can sign out of Game Center, or change what it shares, in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications (optional)

FOLD can show one gentle reminder a day about the Daily Discovery. This is off until you turn on Gentle daily reminder in the game's Settings, at which point iOS asks for your permission.

The reminder is a local notification. It is scheduled on your own device by iOS and does not use a server, a push notification service or any identifier. You can turn it off at any time in the game's Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Information from Apple

Apple may share aggregated, anonymous statistics about the app with us, such as the number of downloads, along with diagnostic and crash reports. Apple only includes people who have agreed in their device settings to share analytics with app developers. This information cannot be used to identify you, and you control it in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security.

Children's privacy

FOLD contains no advertising, no purchases and no chat or social features of its own. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent age where you live, or from anyone else: the game does not collect personal information at all.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of FOLD, please write to us at the address below.

This website

foldgame.app is a simple static website. It sets no cookies, uses no browser storage, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host receives the technical information needed to deliver a page, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. We do not receive those logs.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you describe. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Your choices and rights

Because the game does not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Everything FOLD saves is on your own device or in your own iCloud, where you are in control of it:

  • choose Reset all progress in the game's Settings to erase your saved game;
  • delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone;
  • manage or delete iCloud data in the iPhone Settings app.

If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence deleted, tell us and we will delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. You can exercise them by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If FOLD ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. The current version is always available at foldgame.app/privacy.
