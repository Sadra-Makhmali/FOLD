Playing

How do I fold the sheet?

Touch the sheet and drag it over itself, in the direction you want to fold. A crease appears halfway between where you touched and where your finger is now, and it settles onto the nearest line of the grid with a soft tap. The flap lifts and follows your finger.

Let go once the flap is past halfway and the fold completes. Let go earlier and the sheet relaxes back with nothing changed.

What is the goal?

Bring lights of the same colour together. When two or more lights of the same colour end up on top of each other, they join. Join every light on the sheet to finish the puzzle. The sheet is translucent, so lights join whichever layer they are on.

Some puzzles add a goal of their own: finish within a number of folds, or join several pairs with a single fold (a chain).

Why won't the sheet fold where I want?

Every fold has to follow the rules of the paper. When a fold is refused, the sheet resists and the game shows you the reason. The usual ones are:

  • The fold would leave the frame. The whole sheet must stay inside the frame.
  • A rooted piece is in the way. Rooted parts of the sheet are pinned to the board and cannot be lifted.
  • The crease is sealed or one-way. A sealed seam cannot be folded, and a one-way crease folds in one direction only.
  • There is no paper on one side. A crease needs paper on both sides of it.

If you would rather not drag, turn on Fold from a list in Settings to see every fold that is possible right now.

Is there a timer, or a way to lose?

No. There is no timer, no lives and no energy. If you reach a position that cannot be finished, FOLD tells you and offers to unfold.

Hints and undo

How do hints work?

Tap the lightbulb button during a puzzle. FOLD works out a good next fold from exactly where you are and shows it on the sheet.

Hints are free and unlimited. There is nothing to buy or earn, and using a hint never stops you from finishing a level. One achievement, Quiet Mind, is for solving ten levels without one.

How do I undo a fold or start a puzzle again?

Tap the undo button (the curved arrow) to take back your last fold. You can undo as many times as you like, all the way back to the start of the puzzle.

The restart button (the circular arrow) puts the sheet back exactly as the puzzle began. Neither button costs anything.

Saving and restoring progress

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, automatically, as you play. There is no account to create. If iCloud is turned on for FOLD, your progress can also be kept in your own private iCloud.

How do I restore my progress on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?

  1. Sign in on the iPhone with the same Apple Account you used before.
  2. Check that iCloud is turned on for FOLD: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and look for FOLD among the apps that use iCloud.
  3. Install FOLD and open it while connected to the internet. Your world should return after a moment.

Restoring your iPhone from a backup also brings your progress back.

If iCloud was off for FOLD and there was no backup, progress that existed only on the old device cannot be recovered. We never receive your saved game, so we do not have a copy to give back.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting FOLD removes the progress stored on that iPhone. If your progress was also saved to iCloud or included in a backup, you can restore it as described above.

How do I start over?

Open Settings in FOLD (the gear button) and choose Reset all progress. This erases your world, origami, memories and achievements, and it cannot be undone.

Accessibility

What accessibility options does FOLD have?

Open Settings in FOLD (the gear button). Under Accessibility you will find:

  • Shape symbols on lights. Draws a different shape on each colour of light (drop, petal, star, leaf, moon), so you never need to tell lights apart by colour alone. It is on by default.
  • High contrast. Makes the board clearer and easier to read.
  • Reduce motion. Calms transitions and effects.
  • Fold from a list. Adds a Folds button that lists every fold that is possible, as an alternative to dragging.

Under Sound & touch, Sound effects, Music and Haptics can each be switched off on their own.

Does FOLD work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Buttons, levels and Lumi's messages are labelled, and when VoiceOver is on the list of possible folds is always available, so you can play without the drag gesture.

If any part of the game is hard to use with assistive technology, please tell us. We treat that as a bug.

Game Center and reminders

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional and stays off until you choose Connect Game Center in FOLD's Settings. Once connected, the achievements you earn in FOLD also appear in your Game Center profile. Game Center is run by Apple.

How do I disconnect Game Center?

Game Center sign-in is managed by iOS. Open the iPhone Settings app and choose Game Center to sign out or to change what it shares.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open Settings in FOLD and switch Gentle daily reminder on or off. The first time you turn it on, iOS asks for permission to show notifications.

If the switch will not stay on, notifications for FOLD are probably turned off: open the iPhone Settings app, choose Notifications, then FOLD, and allow them. The reminder comes from your own device once a day. FOLD never sends anything else.

About the game

Is FOLD really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no timers. The whole game is included.

Does FOLD need an internet connection?

No. The story, the Endless Weave and the Daily Discovery all work offline. A connection is only used by features you switch on yourself that depend on Apple's services, such as Game Center or iCloud.

Which devices does FOLD support?

FOLD is made for iPhone and requires iOS 26 or later. The game is in English.

Reporting a bug

Something looks wrong. What should I try first?

Close FOLD completely and open it again, and check that your iPhone is running the latest version of iOS and of FOLD. Your progress is saved after every puzzle, so closing the game does not lose it.

How do I report a bug?

Email sadramakhmali.sm@gmail.com and include as much of this as you can:

  • what happened, and what you expected to happen;
  • where it happened: the realm and level name, or the Endless Weave, or the Daily Discovery and its date;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version (iPhone Settings, then General, then About);
  • your version of FOLD (iPhone Settings, then General, then iPhone Storage, then FOLD);
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can make one.

Ideas and feedback are just as welcome as bug reports.
