Long

About 240 words

FOLD: Lumi's Paper World is a calm origami puzzle journey for iPhone.

Each puzzle is a single translucent sheet scattered with coloured lights. Grab the sheet and fold it over itself: the crease follows your finger, settles onto the grid with a soft tap, and the flap lifts like real paper. When two lights of the same colour meet, they join. Join them all and the sheet folds itself into an origami figure, perhaps a crane, a boat, a lantern or a fox, which travels to a floating island and stays there. Bridges, shrines and trees rise from faint outlines into full colour as you play.

Guided by Lumi, a small spirit made of light, you follow the story through three realms: The Forgotten Garden, The Crystal Ocean and The Sleeping Forest. Together they hold 45 hand-tuned puzzles and 12 chapters, with new ideas along the way, from torn paper and one-way creases to sealed seams, rooted paper and chains of light. Beyond the story, the Endless Weave offers unlimited generated expeditions, and the Daily Discovery gives every player the same small puzzle each day.

FOLD is free and quiet by design. There are no ads, no purchases, no timers and no accounts. It collects no data and plays fully offline. Hints are unlimited, every fold can be undone, and accessibility options include shape symbols on lights, high contrast, reduced motion and folding from a list with full VoiceOver support.

Key features

  • One tactile gesture: fold a sheet of paper with a live, physical-feeling hinge.
  • Every solved puzzle becomes an origami figure that lives on in a persistent 3D world.
  • A complete first story arc across three realms, 45 puzzles and 12 chapters, with a Memory Atlas to fill.
  • Endless Weave: unlimited generated expeditions, each puzzle verified as solvable.
  • Daily Discovery: one shared puzzle a day, entirely optional.
  • Free, with no ads, purchases, timers, accounts or data collection. Fully offline.
  • Unlimited hints and undo, and accessibility options for vision, motion and touch.
